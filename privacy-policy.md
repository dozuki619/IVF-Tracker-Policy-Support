# Privacy Policy for IVFTracker : Cycle & Meds (IVFTracker)

**Effective date:** July 24, 2026

IVFTracker ("the app," "we," "our") is developed by Thomas Silver. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use IVFTracker, available on the Apple App Store.

## The short version

IVFTracker does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or medical information on any server. Everything you enter - medications, appointments, lab results, embryo records, and journal entries — stays on your device. We have no account system, no cloud sync, and no way to see your data, because it never leaves your phone.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect any personal, health, or usage data.** Specifically:

- **No account or sign-in.** IVFTracker doesn't require or offer account creation, so we never collect a name, email, or password.
- **No cloud storage or sync.** All data you enter — medication logs, appointments, lab results, embryo tracking, and journal entries — is stored locally in the app's storage on your device only. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes this information.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind. We do not know how you use the app, how often you open it, or what you enter into it.
- **No advertising.** IVFTracker shows no ads and shares no data with ad networks.

## Information Apple may collect

Apple, as the App Store operator, independently collects limited technical information (such as purchase records and aggregate, anonymized crash/performance diagnostics) as part of operating the App Store and processing your one-time purchase. This collection is governed by [Apple's own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not by us - we do not have access to it, and it is outside our control. If you enable "Share With App Developers" for analytics in your iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymized crash data with us; we do not otherwise receive any information from Apple about your use of the app.

## Health and medical information

IVFTracker is designed to help you track sensitive, personal medical information related to fertility treatment. Because all of this data is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted anywhere by the app, we never have access to it, cannot see it, and cannot disclose it to any third party — including us. You are solely responsible for the physical security of your device and any backups you choose to create.

## Data retention and deletion

Since we never receive your data, we have nothing to retain or delete on our end. Your data remains on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all locally stored data, unless you have separately backed it up (for example, through your device's own backup system) — in which case that backup is governed by the terms of whatever backup service you used, not by us.

## Children's privacy

IVFTracker is not directed at, marketed to, or intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction) — consistent with the rest of this policy, we do not collect information from any user of any age.

## Third-party sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your information to any third party, because we do not collect or have access to it in the first place.

## Your rights

Because IVFTracker does not collect or store your data on any server we control, there is no remote copy of your information for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. You have full control over your data at all times through the app itself and your device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes - for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional feature such as backup or sync - we will update this page and revise the effective date above before that feature ships. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
