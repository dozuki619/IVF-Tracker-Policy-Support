# IVFTracker Support

Need help with IVFTracker? Email thsdevelop20@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

**Common questions**

*Is my data backed up anywhere?* No - IVFTracker stores everything only on your device, with no account or cloud sync. If you're switching phones, use your device's own backup/transfer tools (e.g. iCloud device backup or Quick Start) before setting up the new one, since we have no copy of your data to restore from.

*Does IVFTracker require an internet connection?* No, the app works fully offline.

*How do I request a feature or report a bug?* Email us at the address above with as much detail as you can — what happened, what you expected, and your iOS/device version if it's a bug.

For information on what data the app collects (in short: none, everything stays on your device), see our Privacy Policy, https://dozuki619.github.io/IVF-Tracker-Policy-Support/privacy-policy
